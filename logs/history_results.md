  time because that version was only run without sanitizers; it was found by this re-verification.
  It is kept unchanged as a historical record. Every golfed version from 004 on passes.

  but with 3 colours it *stops* and prints 6, because its fixed caps cut the search short. This
  is exactly why it was rejected ("it has to work") and replaced by the no-limits 1134-char version.
